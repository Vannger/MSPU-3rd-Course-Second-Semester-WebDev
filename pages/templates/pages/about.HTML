What is the Arcane Compendium?

This compendium is a reference guide for spellcasters of all disciplines. Browse, search, and filter spells by school, level, and class — whether you are a Wizard memorising incantations or a Cleric seeking divine favour.

"Magic is not merely a tool — it is a language, spoken between the will of the caster and the fabric of reality itself."

How to Use It

Navigate to the home page to browse featured spells, or use the search and filter tools to find exactly the spell you need. Each entry includes casting time, range, components, duration, and a full description.
